## 🚀 5-STEP QUICK START

### **STEP 1: Deploy Your Website** (2 hours)
Follow `DEPLOYMENT_GUIDE.md` to deploy to cPanel

### **STEP 2: Google Search Console** (15 minutes)
```
1. Go to: https://search.google.com/search-console
2. Click "Add Property"
3. Enter your domain: jewelscalvaryschools.com
4. Verify ownership (choose HTML tag method)
5. Submit sitemap: virtuakids.com.ng/sitemap.xml
6. Done! Google will start indexing your site
```

### **STEP 3: Google My Business** (30 minutes)
```
1. Go to: https://business.google.com
2. Click "Manage now"
3. Enter business details:
   Name: Jewels of Calvary Schools
   Category: School
   Address: 26B, Bamidele Olaifa Street, Igando, Lagos
   Phone: 08060941945
   Website: virtuakids.com.ng
   Hours: Mon-Fri 7:30 AM - 4:00 PM
4. Upload photos (all 6 hero images)
5. Verify your business
6. Done! You'll appear on Google Maps
```

### **STEP 4: Social Media** (1 hour)
Create profiles on:
- Facebook: facebook.com/jewelscalvaryschools
- Instagram: instagram.com/jewelscalvaryschools
- Twitter: twitter.com/jewelscalvary
- YouTube: youtube.com/@jewelscalvaryschools
